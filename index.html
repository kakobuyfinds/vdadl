What to check before ordering

  1. Match the source listing

    Open the CAFANS product page and look for CSSBuy under Buy Link. Confirm the agent page shows the intended seller item and variant. Save the original listing URL so you can find it again if the agent link changes.

  2. Compare actual measurements

    For shoes, check which sizing system and measurement the seller uses. For hoodies and jackets, compare garment measurements against an item you own. Record the chosen size and color in your shortlist.

  3. Review your specific parcel

    When warehouse QC photos are available, compare the labels, color and measurements with your order before forwarding. Confirm the packed dimensions and review the shipping quote and return terms before approving dispatch.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product list that helps shoppers shortlist items before opening them through CSSBuy. This guide uses CAFANS product pages as the starting point.

What do the USD prices include?

The table shows estimated item prices. The final variant price, shipping, payment fees and any taxes or duties affect what you pay.

What if the CSSBuy link does not open?

Check the original seller listing from the CAFANS product page. If it is still available, try opening the original link in CSSBuy and confirm the seller and variant before proceeding.
